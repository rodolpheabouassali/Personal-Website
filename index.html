Research

My research field is differential geometry, with a particular interest in spectral geometry. My PhD project focuses on the biharmonic Steklov problem with Neumann boundary conditions for differential forms.
I am part of the ANR Orbiscar project: orbifolds, scalar curvature, and rigidity. I am also member at the SMF.
